## Summary — N-able Cove Data Protection Campaign

UX Manager position for N-able's Cove Data Protection in Warsaw (hybrid). Player-coach role combining hands-on design work with team leadership for UX designers and researchers. Focus on customer advocacy, continuous discovery, and transforming complex technical problems into unexpectedly simple user experiences.

## Key Responsibilities — N-able UX Manager

### Team Leadership
- Manage a team of UX Designers and Researchers—providing guidance and supporting professional development
- Build outstanding teams—manage relationships and communications, facilitate collaboration, fill knowledge gaps
- Provide leadership, direction, and mentorship for distributed team members
- Oversee day-to-day activities when necessary—help with task delegation, provide feedback

### Hands-On Design Work (Player-Coach)
- Create customer journeys, wireflows, and prototypes—design user flows and interfaces supporting cross-work stream experiences
- Design wireframes and interactive prototypes in Figma to visualize solutions
- Conduct UX reviews to ensure quality and usability of Cove is maintained
- Stay hands-on as designer when needed—comfortable being more hands-on from time to time

### Cross-Functional Collaboration
- Build strong relationships with Engineering, Product Management, and Architecture team members and leaders
- Essential to drive alignment and work toward shared goals/outcomes
- Advocate for user-centered design thinking with continuous discovery mindset
- Ensure success of product releases through proper prioritization, efficient design processes, and strong integration within product development lifecycle

## UX Process & Continuous Discovery

### User-Centered Design Approach
- User-centered design thinking with continuous discovery mindset embedded throughout product development
- Evidence-based problem framing with customer research, analytics, and support signals
- Customer journey mapping to visualize user flows and identify friction points
- Stakeholder collaboration with PM, Engineering, and Architecture teams from discovery onward

### Design & Prototyping
- Create wireframes, wireflows, and interactive prototypes in Figma to visualize user flows and interfaces
- Right fidelity for the decision—sketches for concepts, clickable prototypes for validation
- User testing with real Cove customers (MSPs, IT admins) to validate assumptions
- Leverage Apex Design System components where possible to accelerate delivery

### Quality & Delivery
- Conduct UX reviews to ensure quality and usability of Cove is maintained
- Component-driven design specs with states, edge cases, and accessibility considerations
- Embedded in agile development—planning, standups, reviews—not handoff culture
- Proper prioritization and efficient design processes integrated into product development lifecycle

### Team Process & Continuous Improvement
- Drive improvements to team processes—streamlining workflows and removing bottlenecks
- Manage operational health—team capacity, prioritization, sustainable delivery pace
- Foster collaboration and knowledge sharing across the team
- Balance hands-on design work with team leadership responsibilities
