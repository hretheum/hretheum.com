## Summary — Bank BPH — Digital Conversion Transformation
Redesigned end-to-end digital conversion paths for onboarding and transactions, pairing workflow redesign with analytics to raise conversion in a regulated environment.

## Problem — Bank BPH — Digital Conversion Transformation
Funnel friction across onboarding and transactions depressed conversion, created hotline spikes, and exposed compliance risk.

## Approach — Bank BPH — Digital Conversion Transformation
- Mapped end-to-end customer journeys, re-sequencing steps and prototyping streamlined workflows anchored in advanced wireframing heuristics.
- Ran iterative lab validation and live funnel instrumentation to quantify impact and keep stakeholders aligned on prioritization.

## Stakeholder Management — Bank BPH — Digital Conversion Transformation
- Partnered with compliance, product, and operations leaders to translate research findings into an executable backlog.

## Metrics & Experiments — Bank BPH — Digital Conversion Transformation
- Conversion funnel tracking, task-based testing, and instrumentation of key KPIs to measure lift across cohorts.

## Results & Impact — Bank BPH — Digital Conversion Transformation
- Higher digital conversion, shorter task completion time, calmer support queues, and a strategic UX playbook for continuous experimentation.

## Learnings — Bank BPH — Digital Conversion Transformation
- Strategic UX interventions tied to analytics unlock long-term conversion gains.
