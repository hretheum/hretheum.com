## Summary — Polska Times — Publishing Platform
Designed a digital-first publishing platform that aggregated and syndicated content from 16 regional titles while supporting national newsroom needs.

## Problem — Polska Times — Publishing Platform
Legacy newsroom workflows and tooling were optimized for print, not real-time digital publishing or cross-regional syndication.

## Approach — Polska Times — Publishing Platform
- Mapped end-to-end editorial workflows, highlighting handoffs, approvals, and localization requirements across regions.
- Designed CMS interfaces with role-based permissions, tagging taxonomy, and distribution controls for local vs. national desks.
- Facilitated research and co-creation sessions with editors to validate new processes and tooling.

## Stakeholder Management — Polska Times — Publishing Platform
- Worked directly with editorial leadership, regional editors, and IT to align governance, rollout sequencing, and training.

## Metrics & Experiments — Polska Times — Publishing Platform
- Measured publishing cadence, syndication rates, and newsroom adoption; piloted telemetry on multi-desk collaboration.

## Results & Impact — Polska Times — Publishing Platform
- Digitally native publishing model that accelerated syndication and supported simultaneous regional + national news growth.

## Learnings — Polska Times — Publishing Platform
- Structured governance and taxonomy unlock shared platforms without losing local nuance.
- Clear interfaces between editorial roles and tooling speed execution.
