## Summary — T-Mobile — E‑shop Conversion Paths
Rebuilt T-Mobile’s e-shop conversion flows through research-led prototyping, analytics-led iteration, and alignment rituals that sustained growth experiments.

## Problem — T-Mobile — E‑shop Conversion Paths
High drop-offs in checkout due to unclear steps, misaligned copy, and a bloated upgrade decision tree across new and returning customers.

## Approach — T-Mobile — E‑shop Conversion Paths
- Mapped the end-to-end funnel, prototyped critical upgrade/new-customer branches, and ran moderated tests with contract upgraders.
- Harmonized messaging and removed redundant steps via copy, sequencing, and incentive experiments coordinated with marketing and product.
- Established a shared telemetry dashboard so product, growth, and IT iterated from the same conversion signals.

## Stakeholder Management — T-Mobile — E‑shop Conversion Paths
- Embedded with marketing, product, analytics, and IT to ensure experiments and findings were quickly operationalized.

## Metrics & Experiments — T-Mobile — E‑shop Conversion Paths
- Funnel instrumentation, copy experiments, and segmentation dashboards to quantify lift across cohorts.

## Results & Impact — T-Mobile — E‑shop Conversion Paths
- Increased completed purchases, faster checkout, and repeatable learnings that informed future telecom campaigns.

## Learnings — T-Mobile — E‑shop Conversion Paths
- Clarity and fewer steps beat cosmetic changes.
