<SectionTitle title="DISTRIBUTED TEAM EMPOWERMENT" subtitle="Building High-Performing Design Teams Across Time Zones" />

## Design Leadership in a Distributed World

At N-able, I will build a product design team that doesn't just execute—it influences. Here's how I approach distributed team empowerment and design-driven product development:

### **Individual Contributor Growth**

- **Career Laddering**: Clear progression paths from IC to Senior to Lead, with explicit competencies, outcomes, and coaching checkpoints.
- **Craft Development**: Regular critiques, design challenges, and portfolio reviews that raise the bar on craft and strategic thinking.
- **Visibility Beyond Squads**: Create opportunities for designers to present work to leadership, contribute to Apex Design System, and shape product strategy—not just execute tickets.
- **Skill Diversification**: Mentorship programs, paired design sessions, and rotations across product lines (Cove, RMM, MSP Manager) to broaden skills and empathy.

### **Team Rituals & Culture**

- **Distributed-First Rituals**: Weekly design critiques, bi-weekly knowledge shares, monthly all-hands that work across time zones—with async artifacts (Loom walkthroughs, Notion updates) for those who can't join live.
- **Decision Transparency**: Decision logs, design RFCs, and recorded rationale so remote team members stay aligned and can influence decisions asynchronously.
- **Psychological Safety**: Create a culture where designers feel safe to challenge assumptions, propose bold ideas, and fail fast—without fear of political fallout.
- **Celebration & Recognition**: Public shoutouts, case study showcases, and impact metrics that highlight individual and team contributions to product outcomes.

### **Design System Influence (Apex)**

At N-able, Apex Design System is the connective tissue across products. I will ensure the Cove design team doesn't just consume Apex—they actively shape it:

- **Contribution Model**: Establish clear pathways for designers to propose new patterns, components, and governance updates to Apex—with templates, review rituals, and rollout criteria.
- **Feedback Loops**: Regular sync with the Platform team to surface gaps, edge cases, and usability issues in Apex components—backed by usage data and user research.
- **Pattern Advocacy**: Champion Cove-specific patterns (e.g., data protection workflows, backup configuration, disaster recovery UX) for inclusion in Apex, ensuring they scale across N-able products.
- **Governance Participation**: Embed Cove designers in Apex governance rituals—design reviews, roadmap planning, versioning decisions—so they have a voice in system evolution.

### **Design-Driven Product Development**

Design will be a strategic driver, not a service function. Here's how I embed design influence across the product lifecycle:

#### **Discovery**
- **Evidence-First**: User interviews, support signals, analytics, and competitive analysis to surface opportunities and size problems before jumping to solutions.
- **Problem Framing**: Translate discovery into clear problem statements, success criteria, and measurable outcomes aligned with business goals.
- **Stakeholder Alignment**: Early involvement of PM, engineering, and leadership in discovery—with shared artifacts (journey maps, opportunity canvases) to build buy-in.

#### **Prototyping & Validation**
- **Right Fidelity**: Sketches for concept validation, clickable flows for usability, coded spikes for technical feasibility—at the speed the decision requires.
- **User Testing**: Moderated and unmoderated sessions with real customers (MSPs, IT admins, end users) to validate assumptions and de-risk scope.
- **Design System Alignment**: Ensure prototypes leverage Apex components where possible, surfacing gaps early so Platform team can address them.

#### **Delivery**
- **Component-Driven Specs**: Design handoffs with component specs, states, data requirements, edge cases, and accessibility checks—integrated into engineering workflows (Storybook, Figma Dev Mode).
- **Tight Interfaces**: Embedded in agile rituals (planning, standups, reviews, retros) with PM and engineering—not a handoff culture, but a collaboration culture.
- **Quality Rituals**: Design QA reviews, definition-of-done checklists, and pre-launch audits to ensure shipped work meets design intent and quality standards.

#### **Measurement & Iteration**
- **Instrumentation**: Define HEART/GSM metrics (task success, error rate, time-on-task, adoption, satisfaction) and work with engineering to instrument them in dashboards.
- **Continuous Discovery**: Post-launch research, support signal monitoring, and experimentation to iterate based on observable outcomes—not opinions.
- **Impact Storytelling**: Translate design outcomes into business impact (revenue, retention, support reduction, velocity) to build credibility and secure resources for future bets.

#### **Maintenance**
- **Design Debt Management**: Regular audits of shipped experiences to identify inconsistencies, accessibility gaps, and opportunities for consolidation.
- **Evergreen Patterns**: Maintain design system contributions, update documentation, and evolve patterns as product and user needs change.
- **Knowledge Sharing**: Document learnings, patterns, and playbooks so the team (and broader N-able design org) can build on shared knowledge.

### **Organizational Visibility**

I will ensure the Cove design team has impact and visibility beyond their immediate squads:

- **Executive Storytelling**: Quarterly business reviews with design impact metrics, case studies, and strategic recommendations for product leadership.
- **Cross-Product Collaboration**: Facilitate design syncs with RMM, MSP Manager, and other N-able product lines to share learnings, patterns, and opportunities for convergence.
- **External Presence**: Support designers in publishing case studies, speaking at conferences, and contributing to design community—building N-able's design brand and attracting top talent.
- **Recruitment & Onboarding**: Build scalable hiring systems (structured interviews, design challenges, portfolio reviews) and onboarding playbooks to grow the team without sacrificing quality.
