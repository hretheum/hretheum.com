# Senior Product Designer - Test Company

## About the Role
We're looking for an experienced Product Designer to join our growing design team. You'll be responsible for designing user-centered digital products that solve real problems for our customers in the FinTech space.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of product design experience
- Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design process
- Expert knowledge of Figma, Sketch, and Adobe Creative Suite
- Experience with React and TypeScript for design system work
- Excellent communication and presentation skills
- Bachelor's degree in Design, HCI, or related field

## Responsibilities
- Lead design system initiatives and maintain component libraries
- Collaborate closely with engineering and product teams
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Mentor junior designers and provide design feedback
- Present design concepts to stakeholders
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups

## Nice to Have
- Experience with design tokens and CSS-in-JS
- Knowledge of accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1)
- Background in front-end development
- Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments

## About Us
We're a fast-paced FinTech startup building the future of digital payments. Our team values collaboration, innovation, and continuous learning. We offer competitive compensation, flexible work arrangements, and opportunities for professional growth.

## Benefits
- Competitive salary and equity package
- Health insurance and wellness programs
- Flexible remote work options
- Professional development budget
