## We are currently looking for an inspired **Lead UX/UI Designer** who thrives on solving complex problems and delivering sleek, intuitive, and visually stunning interfaces. At Efigence, you will take the lead in shaping **innovative and complex digital products** for our strategic client in KSA.

# **Your Responsibilities:**

🔹Define and **own the holistic design vision** from ideation to execution

🔹Lead and mentor a **design team, ensuring alignment with product strategy and business goals** 

🔹Create and communicate design decisions via **wireframes, user flows, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups**

🔹Present and **pitch design concepts** to clients and internal stakeholders

🔹**Facilitate workshops** with business, analysts, and tech teams to translate requirements into user-centered solutions

🔹Promote **consistency and quality** across the team’s design work

🔹Support your team’s **performance and professional growth** through active leadership and feedback

🔹Ensure **consistency and quality** in the team’s design work 

 

# Who Are We Looking For?

✅ 7+ years of UX/UI design experience, including team leadership roles

✅Strong portfolio showing work on **large-scale, complex enterprise projects**

**✅In-depth domain knowledge in investments and wealth management**

**✅International professional experience**

**✅A proven track record in team leadership and organizational transformation**

✅ Fluency in **English (C1 level)**

✅ Proven understanding of **digital banking and financial services**

✅ Excellent storytelling, communication, and stakeholder management skills
