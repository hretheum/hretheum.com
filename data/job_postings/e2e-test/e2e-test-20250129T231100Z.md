# Senior Product Designer - E2E Test

## About the Role
We're looking for an experienced Product Designer to join our team and help shape the future of our products.

## Requirements
- 5+ years of product design experience
- Strong portfolio demonstrating end-to-end design process
- Expert knowledge of Figma, Sketch, and Adobe Creative Suite
- Experience with React and TypeScript for design system work
- Excellent communication and presentation skills
- Bachelor's degree in Design, HCI, or related field

## Responsibilities
- Lead design system initiatives and maintain component libraries
- Collaborate closely with engineering and product teams
- Conduct user research and usability testing
- Mentor junior designers and provide design feedback
- Present design concepts to stakeholders
- Create wireframes, prototypes, and high-fidelity mockups

## Nice to Have

- Knowledge of accessibility standards (WCAG 2.1)
- Background in front-end development
- Experience working in Agile/Scrum environments
