### Job description

At pawaPass, we’re not just building a product - **we’re designing the foundation of trusted digital identity in Africa**. Our platform powers KYC, verification, and onboarding for emerging markets across the continent, with real-world applications in fintech, betting, mobile wallets, and more.

We’ve entered a new phase: fresh funding, new hires, and a platform that’s being rebuilt from the ground up. We're looking for a **Senior UX/UI Product Designer** to lead this transformation - to evolve and unify how trust, security, and simplicity are felt across millions of user interactions.

This is not a "design ticket" job.This is a **strategic, foundational design role** - with room to reshape visual identity, UX patterns, and the product experience from day one.



**About the role:**

- You’ll be the **lead Product Designer** across:

  - Our **customer-facing web app**, used for identity verification
  - Our **partner-facing dashboard**, used by B2B clients to manage verifications, API access, and more

- You’ll inherit a design system - but only briefly. Your first challenge will be to **refresh, simplify, and ultimately replace** it with a new design language that balances clarity, trust, and usability.

  

**You’ll collaborate closely with:**

- Tech Lead (Fullstack Developer)
- Frontend Engineer
- Project Manager
- The Group CPTO all deeply involved in product thinking.



This is a role with autonomy, but not isolation. We all care about the product, and we want your ideas at the table.



**What you’ll do:**

- Redesign and evolve the **customer-facing UX** to increase trust, clarity, and conversion

- Bring structure and simplicity to complex **mobile-first and low-bandwidth** experiences

- Create **wireframes, user journeys, clickable prototypes**, and polished UI for high-conversion flows

- Define **design standards, component systems**, and UX guidelines for reuse

- Collaborate with engineers to ensure designs are **feasible, consistent, and fast to implement**

- Support early **wireframing for the admin/partner dashboard**, focused on clarity and performance

- Analyze **Amplitude data, customer interviews**, and market feedback to improve design

- Drive **user research** in collaboration with our partners and local markets

  

**Tooling & Environment:**

- **Design tools:** Figma, Miro, pen&paper (if it works, it works)
- **Data tools:** Amplitude, Customer interviews, Partner feedback loops
- **Product process:** We launch fast, test live, and iterate quickly
- **Tech stack** (for collaboration): React.js, Next.js, Tailwind, Node.js, Prisma



**What we’re looking for:**

You’re a hybrid designer: part UX thinker, part UI artist, all product. You don’t just design screens - you design trust, design flows, and design outcomes.



**You’ll thrive here if you:**

- See constraints (bandwidth, device limitations, user context) as creative fuel
- Can quickly improve existing UX while laying the foundation for a better system
- Know how to balance beauty with brutal simplicity (especially for emerging markets)
- Understand **emerging market challenges** (e.g. mobile-first design, biometric flows, camera permissions)
- Want to contribute to the product strategy, not just design handoffs
- Are opinionated on UX, but flexible enough to ship and iterate
- Love collaborating with engineers and value **clarity over cleverness**



**You’ll need:**

- 5+ years designing **product UX/UI** (mobile flows, dashboards)
- Expert-level **Figma** skills, including components, auto-layout, and responsive design
- Strong portfolio showing **functional design**, not just visual polish
- Deep understanding of **UX best practices and accessibility**
- Ability to build **low-fi wireframes**, clickable prototypes, and high-fidelity UI
- Experience with **data-driven design**, A/B testing, and iteration cycles
- Familiarity with **mobile-first**, offline-friendly, and low-bandwidth design
- Comfortable working closely with **developers, PMs, and even end users**



**Nice to have:**

- Experience designing in **fintech, KYC, or identity platforms**
- Familiarity with **biometric or document scanning UX**
- Prior work in **emerging markets** or regulated industries
- Basic understanding of frontend technologies (you won’t code, but speaking dev helps)



**Why this role is different:**

- You’ll shape the **visual and UX identity** of a product already live in several markets
- You’ll lead design across both **customer and B2B sides** of the platform
- You’ll help define how **trust and identity should feel** in fast-growing economies
- You’ll **own your craft**, with full support from a deeply technical, product-driven team

This is **real impact design**, not just “make it look nice.”You’re solving messy, meaningful problems for users who have limited bandwidth, low-end devices, and a real need for clarity.
