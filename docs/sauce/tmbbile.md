# Hiring Manager Brief — One Portal Tribe (Go‑to‑Market, UX & UI Lead)

## Role Summary
- Lead Go‑to‑Market and UX/UI for One Portal across 10+ markets and ~15 squads.
- Drive measurable outcomes (conversion, ARPU, churn) by aligning UX, product, and engineering around shared metrics.
- Scale ways of working: design tokens, component governance, and outcome‑based roadmaps.

## Why me (relevance & track record)
- Sportradar (2022–2025) — Tribe Product Design Lead across 15+ squads. Built delivery rhythms, mentored designers to senior/lead, aligned with product/engineering.
- ING (2017–2022) — UX Principal Expert/Chapter Lead. Built UX chapter from scratch; laid scalable design system foundations; led digital transformation of legacy platforms.
- Telco proof points — e‑shop conversion work, checkout/flow redesigns, and design ops (tokens/governance) to speed up delivery with consistent UX.

## Telecom outcomes I propose to deliver
- Improve e‑shop conversion for prioritized journeys (device/plan/add‑ons) through prototyping, usability testing, and funnel analysis.
- Establish design tokens and pattern library governance to reduce variance and accelerate cross‑market rollouts.
- Align metrics and decision loops (HEART/North Star + DORA) with Tribe and Markets to focus on outcomes, not artifacts.

## 30‑60‑90 plan (indicative)
- 0–30 days: discovery, alignment with Tribe/Markets; baseline metrics; consent‑gated instrumentation for CTA clicks and web‑vitals; prioritize 2–3 critical journeys.
- 30–60 days: pilot experiments (copy/layout/system variants), validate with data; prepare rollout kit (tokens/patterns, guidance, QA checklist).
- 60–90 days: scale to additional markets; codify governance and contribution model; dashboard outcomes (conversion, ARPU proxies, churn signals).

## What I need from you (first 2 weeks)
- Confirm 2–3 priority journeys and initial target markets (and languages).
- Access to baseline data (checkout funnels, ARPU/churn proxies, CWV), A/B infra (if any), telemetry policies.
- A named decision owner for scope triage; an engineering counterpart for delivery rhythm.
- Consent/CMP policy confirmation (analytics categories, retention) to keep telemetry privacy‑first.
- Contact points per market for rollout readiness (copy, legal, ops).

## Ways of working
- Team Topologies (stream‑aligned, platform, enabling, complicated‑subsystem) applied pragmatically.
- Governance that enables speed: federated contributions, versioned tokens, and clear review gates.
- Privacy‑first telemetry (consent‑gated) to inform decisions without compromising user trust.

## Success metrics (examples)
- +X% conversion on prioritized flows; improved time‑to‑value in rollouts; stable delivery cadence (DORA: deploy frequency, change failure rate, MTTR).
- Reduced UX variance across markets; measurable improvement in NPS/CSAT where applicable.

## Risks & mitigations (snapshot)
- Cross‑market variance slows adoption → mitigate with token/pattern governance and a lean review path.
- Experiment fatigue or low power → pre‑register hypotheses, cap concurrent tests, prioritize high‑leverage surfaces.
- SEO/CWV regressions from UI changes → CI LHCI budgets and RUM dashboards with alerts; fast rollback playbook.

## Stakeholders & cadence
- Weekly Product–Design–Engineering triad (Tribe).
- Market enablement sync (bi‑weekly) for rollout planning and feedback.
- Monthly business review of outcomes (conversion, ARPU proxies, churn signals), with clear next bets.

## Selected telco case highlights
- T‑Mobile e‑shop conversion — checkout/flow redesign with prototyping, usability tests, and funnel analytics. Outcome: improved checkout completion and more stable peak deployments.
- Orange multi‑product conversion — alignment on patterns and flows across offers. Outcome: reduced UX variance and improved consistency at scale.
- Plus.pl platform redesign — IA overhaul and visual system update. Outcome: modernized experience with clearer navigation and purchase paths.

- Location: Warsaw, Mokotów (Hybrid). Engagement: B2B, full‑time. Ready to lead cross‑market coordination with clear KPIs.
